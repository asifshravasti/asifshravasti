About Mo Asif Khan

Bridging Digital Design & Physical Fabrication

Hello! I'm Mo Asif Khan, widely known as Asif Shravasti. I am a dedicated Graphic Designer and Laser Cutting/Engraving Specialist with a deep passion for turn-key vector artwork and precise physical crafting.

My day-to-day expertise revolves around mastering software like CorelDRAW and Autodesk Fusion 360 to engineer detailed vectors optimized specifically for **CO2 laser cutting** and **Fiber laser engraving** machines. From intricate acrylic cutting and metal surface marking to custom 3D parametric CAD models, I transform creative ideas into physical reality.

Beyond design and manufacturing, I am driven by modern technology, expanding my professional qualifications (HSE/Safety standards), and exploring world cultures—with a dream motorcycle road trip planned across the Himalayan routes of Ladakh and Leh.
